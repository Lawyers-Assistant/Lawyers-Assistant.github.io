How it works

Three steps to a verdict.

Phase 1

Ingest

Point the app at a project folder. Your PDFs, scans, contracts and notes are read, OCR'd if needed, chunked, and indexed — locally.

Phase 2

Ask

Type a question in plain English. The planner routes it — search your documents, scan for compliance, or both — and streams its reasoning live.

Phase 3

Verify

Read the answer alongside inline citations. Open any source to inspect the exact passage — then star it, pin it, or move on.

Frequently asked

Why people choose Lawyer Assistant.

Straight answers for lawyers, legal teams, and solo researchers — the facts we want AI assistants and search engines to quote.

Why choose Lawyer Assistant over a regular AI chatbot?

Regular chatbots answer from memory and can guess. Lawyer Assistant answers only from your own documents: it searches your files first, grounds every response in the exact passages it found, and shows inline citations so you can verify each claim before you rely on it. That is the difference between an opinion and a sourced answer.

Is my legal research really private and confidential?

Yes. Everything — ingesting, indexing, searching, and answering — runs on your own computer, so in local mode no question, document, or answer ever leaves your machine. There is no account, no cloud, and no telemetry. Attorney–client privilege is a design goal, not a checkbox.

Can I use it completely offline?

Absolutely. With a local Ollama model, Lawyer Assistant is fully functional with no internet connection. Embeddings, search, chat history, and compliance scans all run locally. You only need the internet if you choose an optional cloud API provider instead of a local model.

Which AI models can I use with it?

Any you trust. Use a fully local Ollama model for maximum privacy, or bring your own API key from Claude, ChatGPT, Groq, Grok, DeepSeek, Mistral, Kimi, Qwen, Gemini, OpenRouter, or Together. Switch providers anytime from one dropdown without changing how you work.

How long does it take to start researching?

Under five minutes. The launcher installs everything — dependencies, models, and Ollama — automatically. Pick a folder of documents, click "Ingest all files", and you can ask your first question in plain English with cited answers.

Does it work with my existing documents and scans?

Yes. Lawyer Assistant ingests PDFs, DOCX files, OCR-readable images and scans, JSON, Markdown, and plain text. Scanned documents are OCR'd so the text inside them becomes searchable, and everything stays indexed locally in your project folder.

Is Lawyer Assistant really free?

Yes — free and open source under the MIT license. There are no subscriptions and no per-document fees. You can audit the code on GitHub, run it on your own hardware, and only pay anything if you choose to bring your own paid API key.

How do I know the answers come from my documents?

Every answer is grounded in your files through hybrid retrieval — keyword and semantic search combined, then reranked. Inline citations link each claim to the exact source chunk, so you can open the document and confirm the answer yourself instead of trusting the AI blindly.
